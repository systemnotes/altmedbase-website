Ginger (Zingiber officinale) for Osteoarthritis: A Meta-Analysis

Reference ginger osteoarthritis joint-pain meta-analysis

Evidence: Grade B (systematic_review)

Summary

Systematic review and meta-analysis evaluating the efficacy of ginger supplementation for reducing osteoarthritis pain and improving function.

Reference Details

Type: study

Authors: Zhang, L., Kim, M., O'Connor, K.

Journal: Journal of Pain Research

Publication Date: 2022-08-10

Volume: 15

Pages: 2145-2159

Abstract

Twelve randomized controlled trials (n=871) were analyzed. Ginger supplementation significantly reduced pain and stiffness in osteoarthritis patients, with no serious adverse effects reported. Authors recommend further large-scale trials.
