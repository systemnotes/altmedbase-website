Acupuncture: Evidence Report and Synthesis (AHRQ)

Reference acupuncture pain-management evidence-review nccih

Evidence: Grade A (observational_study)

Summary

Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality review of acupuncture's efficacy for chronic pain conditions including back pain, osteoarthritis, and headache.

Reference Details

Type: review

Authors: Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality

Journal: AHRQ Evidence Report

Publication Date: 2021-03-01

Abstract

Review of 39 trials found moderate evidence that acupuncture is effective for chronic pain, including back and neck pain, osteoarthritis, and chronic headache, with favorable safety profile.
