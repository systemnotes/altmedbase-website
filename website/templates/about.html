Evidence Grading

All entries include evidence grades and source attribution. Evidence levels range from A (highest) to F (lowest):
GradeLabelMeaning
AHighStrong evidence from high-quality studies
BModerateModerate evidence, some inconsistency
CLowLimited evidence, suggestive but not definitive
DVery LowVery limited evidence, preliminary findings
EAnecdotalAnecdotal or theoretical evidence only
FDisprovenEvidence shows no effect or harm

Methodology

  • Website is generated via website/scripts/generate_site.py
  • Data validation runs via scripts/validate_data.py
