UTI (Urinary Tract Infection)

Ailment uti urinary infection cystitis bacteria bladder

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Infection in any part of the urinary system, commonly affecting the bladder and urethra. Characterized by burning pain, urgency, and frequent urination.

Symptoms

  • burning sensation during urination
  • frequent urge to urinate
  • passing small amounts of urine frequently
  • cloudy or strong-smelling urine
  • pelvic pressure
  • general fatigue
  • low-grade fever (if upper UTI)
