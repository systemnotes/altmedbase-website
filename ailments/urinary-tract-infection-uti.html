Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)

Ailment uti urinary infection cystitis burning bladder

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Infection anywhere in the urinary tract, most commonly in women. Characterized by burning pain during urination and frequent urges.

Symptoms

  • burning pain during urination
  • frequent urge to urinate
  • passing small amounts of urine
  • cloudy urine
  • strong-smelling urine
  • pelvic pain
  • fever and chills (if kidneys involved)
