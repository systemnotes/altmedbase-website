Kidney Issues

Ailment kidney renal fluid-retention edema filtration detox

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Kidney dysfunction affecting fluid balance and waste filtration, leading to fluid retention, edema, and metabolic imbalances.

Symptoms

  • edema (especially in legs, ankles, feet)
  • shortness of breath
  • fatigue
  • poor appetite
  • nausea
  • back pain (below ribs)
  • foamy urine
  • swelling of face or abdomen
