Heart Failure

Ailment heart-failure cardiovascular circulatory edema fluid-retention

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Cardiovascular condition where the heart cannot pump enough blood to meet the body's needs, often causing fluid buildup and edema.

Symptoms

  • shortness of breath
  • fatigue
  • swelling in legs/ankles
  • rapid/irregular heartbeat
  • wheezing
  • coughing/wheezing at night
