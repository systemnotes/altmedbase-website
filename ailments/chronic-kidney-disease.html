Chronic Kidney Disease

Ailment kidney renal chronic kidney-failure edema fluid-retention

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Progressive kidney dysfunction that can lead to kidney failure, requiring dialysis or transplantation. Often causes severe edema and electrolyte imbalances.

Symptoms

  • fatigue
  • swelling in legs/ankles/face
  • shortness of breath
  • headaches
  • poor appetite
  • chest pain
  • muscle twitches/cramps
  • metallic taste in mouth
