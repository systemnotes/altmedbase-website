Traditional Chinese Acupuncture

Therapy acupuncture tcm pain-relief energy-medicine

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Classical acupuncture based on meridian theory and TCM diagnostics. Uses fine needles at specific points to restore qi flow and balance organ systems.

Type: energy_work

Benefits

  • pain modulation
  • stress reduction
  • improved circulation
  • hormonal regulation
  • immune support

Contraindications

  • bleeding disorders
  • active infection at needle site
  • pregnancy (certain points)
  • pacemaker (electroacupuncture)

Session Duration: 45-60 minutes

Frequency Recommendation: 1-2 sessions per week for 4-8 weeks, then maintenance as needed

Cost Range: $80-150 per session
