Herbal Steam Inhalation (Bastrika)

Therapy herbal-steam respiratory sinus ayurvedic

Evidence: traditional

Summary

Ayurvedic steam therapy using medicated herbs to clear respiratory passages, reduce congestion, and support lung health. Can be customized by dosha.

Type: herbal

Benefits

  • sinus decongestion
  • mucus thinning
  • bronchial relaxation
  • antimicrobial action
  • stress relief

Contraindications

  • high fever
  • acute asthma exacerbation
  • recent facial surgery
  • severe hypertension

Session Duration: 10-15 minutes

Frequency Recommendation: 2-3x/day during acute congestion; 1-2x/week for maintenance
