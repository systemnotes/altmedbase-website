Juniper Berry

Remedy juniper berry urinary kidney diuretic antiseptic uti

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Natural antiseptic and diuretic that supports urinary tract health and helps flush infections, particularly UTIs and kidney issues.

Indications

  • urinary tract infection
  • kidney stones
  • fluid retention
  • bladder issues

Contraindications

  • pregnancy
  • kidney disease
  • hypertension
  • diabetes

Preparations

  • berry
  • tea
  • tincture
  • essential oil (diluted)

Body Systems Affected

  • urinary
  • renal
  • circulatory

Treats These Ailments

Conditions listed as treatable by this remedy:

  • Edema, Fluid Retention
    Excess fluid accumulation in tissues causing swelling, typically in legs, ankles, or abdomen. Can be caused by heart conditions, kidney dysfunction, liver disease, or medication side effects.
  • Bladder Issues
    Bladder dysfunction including urinary retention, urgency, and discomfort, often linked to fluid imbalance and tissue salt deficiencies.
  • Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
    Infection anywhere in the urinary tract, most commonly in women. Characterized by burning pain during urination and frequent urges.
  • Kidney Issues
    Kidney dysfunction affecting fluid balance and waste filtration, leading to fluid retention, edema, and metabolic imbalances.
  • Chronic Kidney Disease
    Progressive kidney dysfunction that can lead to kidney failure, requiring dialysis or transplantation. Often causes severe edema and electrolyte imbalances.
  • Kidney & Bladder Health
    Comprehensive kidney and bladder health covering urinary tract infections, bladder dysfunction, kidney stones, and related conditions affecting fluid balance and toxin removal.
  • UTI (Urinary Tract Infection)
    Infection in any part of the urinary system, commonly affecting the bladder and urethra. Characterized by burning pain, urgency, and frequent urination.
