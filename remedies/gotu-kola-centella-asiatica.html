Gotu Kola (Centella Asiatica)

Remedy gotu-kola circulation lymphatic wound-healing

Evidence: scientific

Summary

Adaptogenic herb that improves circulation, supports lymphatic drainage, and promotes wound healing; traditionally used for cognitive function.

Indications

  • poor circulation
  • lymphedema
  • wound healing
  • cognitive function
  • varicose veins

Contraindications

  • low blood pressure
