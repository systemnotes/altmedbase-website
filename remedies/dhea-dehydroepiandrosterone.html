DHEA (Dehydroepiandrosterone)

Supplement hormone adrenal anti-aging immune

Evidence: opinion

Summary

Adrenal hormone precursor with anti-aging and immune-modulating properties; declines with age.

Indications

  • adrenal fatigue
  • depression
  • low libido
  • immune deficiency
  • age-related decline

Contraindications

  • pregnancy
  • breastfeeding
  • hormone-sensitive cancers
  • PCOS
  • adolescents

Preparations

  • capsule
  • sublingual
  • topical gel

Body Systems Affected

  • endocrine
  • nervous
  • immune
  • reproductive
