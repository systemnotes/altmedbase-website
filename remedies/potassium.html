Potassium

Mineral mineral electrolyte fluid-balance blood-pressure

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Essential electrolyte that regulates fluid balance, nerve transmission, and muscle contraction.

Indications

  • muscle cramps
  • fatigue
  • irregular heartbeat
  • hypertension
  • edema
  • kidney stones (cystine)

Contraindications

  • kidney disease
  • hyperkalemia
  • certain medications (ACE inhibitors, potassium-sparing diuretics)
