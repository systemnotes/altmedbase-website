Uva Ursi

Remedy uva-ursi bearberry urinary uti bladder antibacterial

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Herbal antibiotic that targets urinary tract infections specifically; increases urine acidity and has direct antibacterial action against E. coli.

Indications

  • urinary tract infection
  • cystitis
  • bladder infection
  • kidney infection

Contraindications

  • kidney disease
  • liver disease
  • prolonged use
  • children under 12

Preparations

  • leaf
  • capsule
  • tincture
  • tea

Body Systems Affected

  • urinary
  • renal
  • immune

Treats These Ailments

Conditions listed as treatable by this remedy:

  • Edema, Fluid Retention
    Excess fluid accumulation in tissues causing swelling, typically in legs, ankles, or abdomen. Can be caused by heart conditions, kidney dysfunction, liver disease, or medication side effects.
  • Bladder Issues
    Bladder dysfunction including urinary retention, urgency, and discomfort, often linked to fluid imbalance and tissue salt deficiencies.
  • Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
    Infection anywhere in the urinary tract, most commonly in women. Characterized by burning pain during urination and frequent urges.
  • UTI (Urinary Tract Infection)
    Infection in any part of the urinary system, commonly affecting the bladder and urethra. Characterized by burning pain, urgency, and frequent urination.
