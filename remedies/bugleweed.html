Bugleweed

Remedy bugleweed lycopus thyroid diuretic edema

Evidence: traditional

Summary

Herb used traditionally to reduce fluid retention, particularly when related to thyroid dysfunction or heart conditions.

Indications

  • edema
  • fluid retention
  • hyperthyroidism
  • palpitations

Contraindications

  • hypothyroidism
  • pregnancy
  • breastfeeding

Preparations

  • tincture
  • tea
  • capsule

Body Systems Affected

  • endocrine
  • circulatory
  • renal

Treats These Ailments

Conditions listed as treatable by this remedy:

  • Edema, Fluid Retention
    Excess fluid accumulation in tissues causing swelling, typically in legs, ankles, or abdomen. Can be caused by heart conditions, kidney dysfunction, liver disease, or medication side effects.
  • Bladder Issues
    Bladder dysfunction including urinary retention, urgency, and discomfort, often linked to fluid imbalance and tissue salt deficiencies.
  • Immune System Weakness
    Weakened immune system leading to frequent infections and slow recovery.
  • Cognitive Decline
    Progressive decline in cognitive function including memory, thinking, and reasoning.
