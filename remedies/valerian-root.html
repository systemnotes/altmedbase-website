Valerian Root

Remedy valerian sleep anxiety nervous-system

Evidence: scientific

Summary

Sedative herb that promotes deep sleep, reduces anxiety, and relaxes muscles; acts on GABA receptors in the brain.

Indications

  • insomnia
  • anxiety
  • stress
  • muscle tension
  • nerve pain

Contraindications

  • pregnancy
  • alcohol (sedation)
  • other sedatives (synergistic effects)

Preparations

  • root
  • tea
  • tincture
  • capsule
  • extract

Body Systems Affected

  • nervous
  • muscular
  • endocrine

Treats These Ailments

Conditions listed as treatable by this remedy:

  • Insomnia & Sleep Disorders
    Sleep disorders characterized by difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or excessive daytime sleepiness, often related to stress and anxiety.
