Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Supplement omega-3 fish-oil inflammation cardiovascular

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Essential fatty acids (EPA and DHA) that support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and promote brain function.

Indications

  • omega-3 deficiency
  • inflammation
  • cardiovascular support
  • brain health
  • high triglycerides

Contraindications

  • fish/shellfish allergy
  • blood thinners (monitor)

  • liquid
  • capsule

Body Systems Affected

  • cardiovascular
  • nervous
  • immune

Treats These Ailments

Conditions listed as treatable by this remedy:

  • Hypertension
    Chronic elevation of blood pressure in the arteries, increasing risk of heart disease, stroke, and kidney failure.
  • Heart Failure
    Cardiovascular condition where the heart cannot pump enough blood to meet the body's needs, often causing fluid buildup and edema.
  • Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)
    Chronic condition characterized by persistently elevated blood pressure in the arteries, increasing risk of cardiovascular events and kidney damage.
  • Liver Cirrhosis
    Chronic liver disease characterized by scarring (fibrosis) of liver tissue, affecting liver function and detoxification.
