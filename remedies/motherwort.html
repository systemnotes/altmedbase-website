Motherwort

Remedy motherwort leonurus calming heart-support edema anxiety

Evidence: traditional

Summary

Herb that calms the nervous system and supports heart function, helpful for edema related to stress or anxiety.

Indications

  • edema
  • anxiety
  • heart palpitations
  • menstrual cramps
  • thyroid support

Contraindications

  • pregnancy
  • breastfeeding
  • low blood pressure
  • bleeding disorders

Preparations

  • herb
  • tea
  • tincture
  • capsule

Body Systems Affected

  • nervous
  • cardiovascular
  • reproductive

Treats These Ailments

Conditions listed as treatable by this remedy:

  • Edema, Fluid Retention
    Excess fluid accumulation in tissues causing swelling, typically in legs, ankles, or abdomen. Can be caused by heart conditions, kidney dysfunction, liver disease, or medication side effects.
  • Bladder Issues
    Bladder dysfunction including urinary retention, urgency, and discomfort, often linked to fluid imbalance and tissue salt deficiencies.
  • Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
    Infection anywhere in the urinary tract, most commonly in women. Characterized by burning pain during urination and frequent urges.
  • Kidney Issues
    Kidney dysfunction affecting fluid balance and waste filtration, leading to fluid retention, edema, and metabolic imbalances.
  • Lymphedema
    Lymphatic system dysfunction causing chronic swelling, usually in arms or legs, when lymph fluid cannot drain adequately.
  • Gallbladder Issues / Gallstones
    Gallbladder dysfunction or gallstone formation causing digestion problems, bloating, and referred pain. Often linked to liver congestion.
