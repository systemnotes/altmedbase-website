Hawthorn Berry

Remedy hawthorn cardiovascular circulation heart-strength

Evidence: peer_reviewed

Summary

Heart tonic herb that supports cardiovascular health by improving blood flow and strengthening heart contractions.

Indications

  • heart failure
  • poor circulation
  • hypertension
  • weak pulse

Contraindications

  • low blood pressure
  • digoxin use (interactions)

Preparations

  • tincture
  • capsule
  • standardized extract
  • tea

Body Systems Affected

  • cardiovascular
  • circulatory

Treats These Ailments

Conditions listed as treatable by this remedy:

  • Hypertension
    Chronic elevation of blood pressure in the arteries, increasing risk of heart disease, stroke, and kidney failure.
  • Heart Failure
    Cardiovascular condition where the heart cannot pump enough blood to meet the body's needs, often causing fluid buildup and edema.
  • Cardiovascular Disease
    Group of diseases affecting the heart and blood vessels, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, and stroke.
